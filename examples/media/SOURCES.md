# Sample media sources

These small fixtures are for local format and multimodal tests.

- `sample-public-domain.jpg`: resized from [Sample 1.JPEG](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sample_1.JPEG), public-domain U.S. government work.
- `sample-public-domain.png`: [Bmp format.png](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bmp_format.png), released to the public domain.
- `sample-public-domain.bmp`: BMP conversion of the public-domain PNG above.
- `sample-speech.wav`: [A'anaa.wav](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:A%27anaa.wav), downloaded from Wikimedia Commons.
- `sample-speech.mp3`: small MP3 conversion of the WAV sample above.

The converted files are intentionally small and are not intended as quality
benchmarks. Check the original source pages for the applicable media terms.
